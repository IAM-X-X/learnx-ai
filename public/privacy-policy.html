Privacy Policy — LearnX AI

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Overview

LearnX AI ("we", "us", or "the app") is an AI-powered learning platform. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

The Short Version

  • No account required. You don't need to sign up to use LearnX AI.
  • Your study data stays on your device. Study sets, flashcards, quizzes, notes, and podcasts are stored locally.
  • Ads are shown on the free tier. Premium subscribers ($5.99/month) see no ads.
  • Profile data (name/nickname, education level, subjects) is stored locally on your device.
  • No personal data sold. We never sell your data to anyone.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Study set titles, descriptions, and source content (text you paste, upload, or provide via URL)
  • AI-generated notes, flashcards, quizzes, and podcast scripts
  • Your study progress, quiz scores, and spaced-repetition scheduling
  • Chat conversations with the AI tutor
  • Your onboarding profile (name/nickname, education level, selected subjects)
  • App preferences (theme color, daily goal, premium status, dismissed prompts)

All of the above is stored in your browser's local storage or a local SQLite database. It never leaves your device.

Data Sent to AI Services for Processing (Not Stored)

When you use AI features (generating notes, flashcards, quizzes, summaries, podcasts, or chatting with the tutor), the text you provide is sent to our AI processing service to generate responses. This data is processed in real-time and is not stored on any server.

Audio Data (Voice Input)

If you use the voice input feature in the AI Tutor, audio is recorded temporarily in your browser, sent to our speech-to-text service for transcription, and then discarded. Audio is never stored.

Advertising

The free tier of LearnX AI displays ads. We use Google AdSense to serve ads. AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to show you relevant ads based on your interests and browsing activity.

  • What ad companies collect: Google AdSense may collect information such as your IP address, browser type, and browsing behavior to serve personalized ads.
  • Opt out of personalized ads: Visit Google Ads Settings to opt out of personalized advertising.
  • Remove all ads: Subscribe to LearnX AI Premium ($5.99/month) to remove all ads permanently.

During development/testing, placeholder ads are shown. Real AdSense ads only appear when configured with a valid AdSense publisher ID.

Premium Subscription ($5.99/month)

  • Payment processing: Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your credit card information — Stripe handles all payment data securely.
  • What we store: Your premium status (a simple true/false flag) is stored locally on your device.
  • What Stripe stores: Stripe stores your email, payment method, and subscription status. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Cancel anytime: You can cancel your subscription at any time. Ads will reappear when your subscription expires.

Admin Override

LearnX AI includes an admin override feature that allows designated administrators to access premium features (including ad-free) without a paid subscription. This is intended for testing and internal use. Admin access is activated via a secret code stored in the app's source code.

Premium Feature Limits

The free tier has limits on certain AI features. Premium subscribers get unlimited access. The following may be limited on the free tier:

  • Number of AI study set generations per day
  • Number of AI podcast audio generations
  • Daily AI tutor messages

What We Don't Do

  • ❌ We don't collect personal information beyond your name/nickname (which is stored locally only)
  • ❌ We don't use analytics or crash reporting SDKs
  • ❌ We don't sell or share your data with third parties (except ad networks on the free tier)
  • ❌ We don't access your contacts, location, camera, or files (beyond what you explicitly upload)
  • ❌ We don't have user accounts or authentication (except Stripe payment via email)

Permissions Used

  • Microphone (optional): Only when you tap the mic button for voice input in the AI Tutor.
  • Storage: Used to save your study data locally on your device.
  • Network: Used for AI processing, ad serving (free tier), and payment processing (Premium).

Data Security

Your study data is stored locally on your device. Payment data is handled by Stripe (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified). We use HTTPS for all network communication.

Children's Privacy

LearnX AI is designed for learners of all ages, including students in primary and secondary school. We don't collect personal information from anyone. The only data stored is what the user voluntarily enters (name/nickname, study content). No parental consent is needed because no data is collected server-side.

Your Rights

  • Delete your data: Clear your browser data or uninstall the app.
  • Export your data: Use the "Export as JSON" feature on any study set.
  • Cancel subscription: Cancel your Premium subscription at any time via Stripe.
  • Opt out of ads: Subscribe to Premium or use an ad blocker.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please refer to the app's settings or contact the developer.

Open Source
